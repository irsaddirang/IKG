## Suffix forms

| Form | Count | Example | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `CLASS<number>` | 3,613 | `K001`, `X001`, `W5106` | plain device |
| `CLASS<number>:<pin>` | 2,060 | `X001:1`, `Q027:14`, `M102/1:U` | device with an explicit terminal/pin |
| `CLASS<number>/<sub>` | 324 | `+A497/2`, `B007/1`, `F029/1` | nth partial device / sub-assembly |
| `CLASS<number>/<sub>:<pin>` | 120 | `M102/1:U`, `K160/1:A2` | partial device plus pin |

### `+` vs `-` sign

| Sign | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `+A497` | main item that **is** part of this drawing set (e.g. a PC board assembly) | `+A457`, `+A497/1` |
| `-X3.A497` | a **component located on** an assembly; the `.A497` names the host | `-X3.A497`, `-X1.A368:1` |

### Pin / terminal suffixes

The part after `:` is the terminal. Observed forms:

- numeric pins: `X001:15`, `K004:21`, `Q027:14`
- letter pins (motor terminals): `M102/1:U`, `M102/1:V`, `M102/1:W`
- letter pairs (relay contacts / coil): `K160:A2`, `G006:A1`, `G006:T1`
- alpha-numeric pins: `A244:bn`, `X001:U29`

> **Reading tip:** `K160/1:A2` = contact/part **1** of relay `K160`, terminal `A2`.
> The `/1` is not a pin number, it is the sub-device index.
