# Index of potentials (nets)

Named electrical potentials used across the drawings. A potential is electrically one node: every sheet that mentions it is connected.

**Counting rule.** A sheet counts here if it prints the potential either as a standalone label (`24V`) *or* as the head of a dotted wire reference (`24V.X001:15`). Prose mentions such as `SPANNUNGSVERSORGUNG: 24V` or `(24V)` in a title or note are **not** counted, because they name a topic rather than a wire. A document that never prints the potential therefore legitimately shows **0** — that is a measured zero, not missing data.
